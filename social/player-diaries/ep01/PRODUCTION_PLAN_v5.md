# 48 Hours Before Belgium — v5 production plan (the cinematic interview film)

Status: **for owner review.** Nothing in this plan is built yet. After approval it becomes the
implementation plan, built stage by stage with owner review gates.

## 0. The brief (owner, 2026-10-01)

- It must play as **one documentary interview with a story**, not consecutive clips.
- The spine is the **contradiction doc** (chosen): he claims calm in the chair, and the footage betrays him until the sincere ending.
- Production level: a **Hollywood short film**. Cinematic camera, not limited to existing shots.
- **Wider angles and breathing room.** Too many shots are close-ups.
- **Nothing moves by itself.** Every prop that moves is held or pushed by a person.
- **Runtime:** up to 150 s, and it must keep people watching (Instagram + TikTok).
- Locked from earlier: Onyx voice for #9, `Q:` text cards (no interviewer voice), the family chat hook, the father has passed away (said once), the Coach EP02 teaser.

## 1. Platform rules we design to (research)

| Finding | What we do |
|---|---|
| Instagram ranks on **watch time, likes and sends**; sends weigh ~3–5× likes for reach to non-followers. Max Reel length is now **3 min**, but early-watch survival decides distribution. [1][2] | Frame 0 is the hook. Write **two "sendable" moments** (the "he is" chat; "Needs salt." → "Pressure doesn't really affect me.") that people DM to their partner or mum. |
| TikTok: the first 1–3 s decide it. Videos over 60 s need **open loops and re-hooks**; a 2-min video held 60% beats a 30-s one held 100%. Targets for 60 s–3 min: **> 30% average watch**. [3][4] | **Chapters with a re-hook every 18–25 s**: each chapter opens on a new chair question (an open loop) and closes on a laugh or a turn. |
| Micro-docs on Reels are strongest at 45–90 s, with drop-off after 90 s. Hook → context → escalating reveals → twist. [5][6] | Two deliverables: **the film (~2:25)** and a **trailer cut (~45 s)**. The trailer goes after cold reach and points to the film. |

## 2. Story

**Logline.** Two days before Türkiye play Belgium, the country's #9 sits down for an interview and
insists he isn't nervous. The footage of his 48 hours disagrees, and so does his seven-year-old son.

**Dramatic question.** *Is he nervous?* It's asked in the chat at 0:03 ("he is"), asked again in the chair
at 0:09, and answered by him at 2:05 ("I've been awake since four").

**Theme.** What fathers pass on: his late father (#9 for Türkiye before him), him, his son.

**Rule of every scene.** It answers or contradicts the question on screen, and it causes the next one.

### Script (chair = interview set, Thursday night; `Q:` = on-screen card; *italic* = his chair answer heard over the footage)

**COLD OPEN**
1. PHONE (5.5 s): *Aile ❤️ 06:46.* Annem: "they said on tv you look tired 😟" · "are you nervous??" · he types "ask belgium.", deletes it, sends "no." · Aşkım: "we're out of salt" · Oğlum: **"he is"**. *(proven hook; frame 0 = POV box)*
2. INTERVIEW ROOM, WIDE (C-cam, 24 mm): his living room by night, re-lit as an interview set. Softbox, C-stand, the monitor glow at frame edge, one chair. A clapper snaps in: "PLAYER DIARIES · EP01 · TAKE 1". He sits. *(authenticity + breathing room)*
3. A-cam MCU. **Q: ARE YOU NERVOUS?** · #9: "No." *(beat)* "I sleep like a baby."
4. SMASH: BEDROOM, 04:00, blue dark. Wide from the doorway: the bed, his eyes open, the clock reading 04:00. Slow push. → Title **48 HOURS BEFORE BELGIUM** burns in over the dark → clock time-lapse 04:00 → 06:47.

**CH 1 · MORNING**
5. 06:47. Wide two-shot from the door: the kid in Dad's #9 at the bedside, ball under his arm. KID: "You're late." · #9: "For what?" · (reverse CU) the ball lifts into frame · #9: "Not today."
6. KITCHEN, WIDE establishing: morning sun through the window, the partner at the counter, him at the stove. *(breathing room)*
7. Chair, B-cam profile: **Q: SPECIAL DIET BEFORE BELGIUM?** · "Eggs."
8. His **hand** takes an egg from the carton → cracks it on the rim (ECU) → it slides into the oil. *"My father made them before every game I ever played."* Slow push on him at the stove, his back to the window. *"He's not here to make them anymore. So I do."*
9. Chair: "And one coffee."
10. MS: he pours and keeps pouring. Her **hand** takes the mug mid-pour; she walks off with it. *"She has never let me finish one."* His eyes follow the mug. → the phone buzzes on the counter. *(re-hook)*

**CH 2 · THE NOISE**
11. Chair: **Q: DO YOU READ WHAT THEY SAY ABOUT YOU?** · "Never."
12. EXTERIOR, crane-down/tracking: his car through the city morning *(new: car exterior + street)*. Interior MS: the radio panel "…and the whole country is asking one question—". His **hand** turns it off.
13. Insert: the dash phone lights up: Annem 🔗🔗🔗. CU: his eyes slide down. He reads every one. *"My mother sends me all of it anyway."*
14. Chair: **Q: WHAT DO FANS ASK YOU FOR?** · "Selfies, mostly."
15. BAKERY: exterior wide (shop front, bell, morning street) → inside wide: he takes the bag; the old man stands, crosses → MCU low: "Two goals." · #9: "Good morning." → from outside the door: "Two!" — the small smile.
16. *"He asked my father for two goals. Thirty years ago."* *(beat)* *"He got one."* *(stakes)*

**CH 3 · THE WORK**
17. Chair: **Q: WHAT HAPPENS FRIDAY?** · "No idea."
18. TRAINING: high crane establishing over the pitch (overcast, long shadows) → laces ECU (his **hands**) → touch → tracking sprint (85 mm) → the keeper sets → **the strike in slow motion** (time remap ×0.35), the ball top corner, the net → he turns away; no celebration. *"This is the only place it goes quiet."*

**CH 4 · THE DEFENDER**
19. LIVING ROOM, afternoon, wide locked (comedy framing): sofa, ice on his knee. The kid places the ball beside him. #9: "Not now." → CU: the kid's stare (no blink) → he gives in (lifts the ice off with his hand, stands).
20. Chair: **Q: TOUGHEST DEFENDER YOU'VE EVER FACED?** *(a long beat)*
21. Play, with proper coverage: wide master (passes) → rug-level nutmeg through his legs → the cushion goal → **the kid's turn-away, framed exactly like the training shot** *(match composition)*. Chair: "…He's seven."
22. The ball clips the lamp → PARTNER (off): "Careful!" → wide: both frozen, each pointing at the other.
23. DINNER, evening wide (warm pendant): he plates with care (**hands**: pan + spoon), carries it to her. "Well?" · CU: she tastes. *(beat)* "Needs salt." · his face, a millimetre.
24. **SMASH** to the chair: **Q: DOES PRESSURE GET TO YOU?** · "Pressure doesn't really affect me." *(the sendable laugh)*

**CH 5 · THE TRUTH**
25. The interview, later: the room darker, only the practical lamp. A-cam begins a slow push that doesn't stop. **Q: LAST ONE. WHAT DOES PLAYING FOR TÜRKİYE MEAN TO YOU?** He looks down. Two seconds of silence; the music drops out.
26. HALLWAY at night, dolly: a framed photo — **his father in the #9 shirt**, decades ago *(new: father identity)*. *"At his age I slept in my father's shirt."*
27. CHILD'S ROOM: the kid asleep in the #9 shirt. Dolly in. *"You feel like everyone's coming with you."* → boots by the door, the packed bag (his **hands** zip it). *"Even when you're playing away."*
28. Chair, CU at the end of the push: **Q: SO… ARE YOU NERVOUS?** · the half-smile · "…I've been awake since four."
29. C-cam wide: the crew light switches off with a click. Darkness. *(the son was right)*

**FINALE · FRIDAY**
30. 19:05 BRUSSELS. The team bus door opens (match cut from his front door closing). Crowd, phone flashes, a long lens across the barrier.
31. TUNNEL: Steadicam follow from behind, one-point symmetry. **Q: STILL NOT NERVOUS?** He stops, looks back over his shoulder: "Ask Belgium." *(the reply he deleted at 0:04)*
32. He walks into the light → white → **HNC LEAGUE**.
33. TEASER: buzz. Coach: "my office. 8am." *(EP02; placeholder copy)*

**Trailer cut (~45 s):** shots 1 → 3 → 4 → 10 → 13 → 15 → 23–24 → 28 → 31 → "Full episode on our page". Built from the same plates; costs only an edit.

## 3. Camera & look bible (Hollywood rules, applied in Blender)

**Shot-size budget per scene:** ≥ 30% wide/full, ~40% medium, ≤ 30% CU/ECU. **Every scene opens on an
establishing wide or a "dirty" medium.** CUs are earned by emotion or a joke's reaction. [12]

**Lens kit:** 18/24 mm (establishing; vertical depth), 35 mm (observational doc standard), 50 mm
(interview A-cam), 85 mm (interview B-cam profile; sport tracking), 135 mm (compression: training,
the bakery through the window, the crowd).

**Interview (the spine): a three-camera setup** [7][13][14]
- **A-cam:** 50 mm MCU, eye level. His eyeline sits just off the lens (the interviewer's seat), never into it.
- **B-cam:** 85 mm profile, 70–90°, same side of the 180° line. Cut to it for rhythm and jokes.
- **C-cam:** 24 mm "the room": the set, the lights, the darkness. Breathing room and documentary truth; used on pauses.
- **Light:** Rembrandt key 45° from the shadow side, warm; cool negative fill; a rim; a warm practical lamp in the background. Warm subject on a cool background for depth.
- **Movement:** locked for comedy. **The only slow push is chapter 5**, motivated by emotion. [15][16]

**Movement grammar by chapter**

| Chapter | Movement |
|---|---|
| Cold open | locked + one slow push in the dark |
| Morning | observational handheld (subtle) |
| Noise | vehicle mounts + one exterior crane |
| Work | sport: tracking pans, crane, slow motion |
| Defender | **locked wides**: comedy reads in stillness |
| Truth | slow dollies |
| Finale | Steadicam follow + one-point perspective |

**Vertical composition:** stage in depth, not width. Three layers in every frame: a "dirty" foreground
(door frame, plant, shoulder), the subject, and a practical-lit background. Use high and low angles. Doorways
frame wides in 9:16. [17][18][19]

**Colour and light arc:**
- 04:00 moonlight blue
- warm morning gold
- neutral day (car, bakery)
- crisp overcast (training)
- golden afternoon (home)
- tungsten warm (dinner)
- interview chiaroscuro (warm key, cool fill), darkening across chapter 5
- Friday steel blue
- white

**Transitions:**
- J-cuts: the chair answer starts before the b-roll.
- Smash cuts for the jokes.
- Match compositions: the training turn-away ↔ the kid's turn-away; the front door ↔ the bus door.

**Blender implementation of the bible** [20][21][22]
- Physical cameras with real focal lengths and DOF. **Focus pulls are keyed** (`Cam.place(focus=…)` already supports rack focus).
- Motion blur at a 180° shutter (already).
- **Dolly, crane and arc moves** are keyed camera paths with easing on the existing `Cam`, giving parallax for depth. No new rig needed. The Blender *Camera Rigs* add-on (dolly/crane) is an option for hand-tuned moves.
- **Handheld** uses the existing noise profiles (`locked`, `subtle`, `observational`, `follow`).
- **Slow motion** uses Blender **time remapping** on the strike shot (`frame_map_old/new`), so the performance and ball physics slow down uniformly.
- The **compositor finish** gets per-location colour balance (the arc above), plus the existing glare, vignette and grain. Optional: a subtle lens distortion / chromatic aberration on the documentary cameras.

## 4. Staging rule: nothing moves by itself

Audit of v4 (confirmed in code):

| Shot | Problem |
|---|---|
| S08_SH01 | The knife chops with no person |
| S08_SH03 | The spoon plates with no person |
| S10_SH01 | The shirt drops into the bag with no person |
| S10_SH02 | The zip slides with no person |

Already fixed in v3/v4: the egg, the carafe, the mug.

**Rule:** every moving prop is either **held** (`hold_prop`, with a **contact guard** that fails the build if the arm
tip is more than 3 cm off) or **pushed** by a body part (kicks, ball physics). Inserts become
**hand-in-frame inserts** driven by the rig. **A build-time check flags any animated prop that isn't held, isn't the
ball, and isn't on an allowlist** (doors, lamp wobble, steam).

## 6. Pipeline upgrades (small, reusable for EP02+)

1. `edit.json` shots gain `cam: A|B|C` for interview coverage, and `remap` for slow motion. The interview builder renders the same performance from any of the three cameras.
2. `sh.contact(p, side, prop, t)`: the reach/contact guard as one helper (replaces today's per-shot ad-hoc guards).
3. A build-time **"floating prop" check** (§4).
4. Per-location colour balance in the compositor finish.
5. **A shot-size audit**: `diaries check` reports the WS/MS/CU share per scene against the §3 budget.
6. **Trailer cut**: a second edit list (`edit-trailer.json`) that reuses the plates.
