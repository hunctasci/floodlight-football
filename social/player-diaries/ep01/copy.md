## ALT TEXT

An animated documentary-style short about a fictional low-poly footballer, Türkiye's number 9, in the two days before a match against Belgium. He drives at night and answers "Are you nervous?" with "Ask Belgium." His child wakes him — "You're late." He cooks eggs and pours too much coffee until his partner takes the cup. He turns off match talk on the car radio. An older supporter at a bakery points at him: "Two goals." At training he strikes the ball into the top corner past a diving keeper. At home his child brings him a football and they play in the living room until they nearly knock over a lamp. His dinner "needs salt." In a dark interview he says playing for Türkiye feels like everyone is coming with you, even away. On matchday he hugs his child goodbye, gets off the team bus past cheering fans, and in the stadium tunnel repeats "Ask Belgium" before walking into the light. Ends on the HNC League logo.
