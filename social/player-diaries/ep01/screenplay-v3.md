## The through-line (what v3 adds)

**What he says on camera vs what he admits in the diary.** On camera, #9 is never nervous: "Ask Belgium." The voice-over gives him away, a little at a time. His father's eggs, the bakery man who asked his father for the same two goals, the kid who scares him. The last voice-over line admits he has been awake since four. The calm man is nervous, and the viewer is the only one who knows. That gap is where the feeling lives. The character needs no mouth because the voice-over is him thinking.

## Caption & text style

- **Frame-0 hook:** a white POV box in the upper safe zone, in the same style as the group-chat reel.
- **Spoken lines:** burned-in, bottom-third safe zone. Speaker cue as a small coloured name tag (NINE / KID / PARTNER / ELDER / INT); INT is never shown on screen.
- **Voice-over:** italic, with no name tag, slightly higher on the screen. It reads as thought, not speech.
- **Chat:** the same phone UI as the group-chat reel (`worlds/phone`), dark mode.
