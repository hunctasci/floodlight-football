HNC League is a free browser football game where you represent your country, play 1v1 matches and contribute to a weekly world table.

Play for your country →

How to start

  1. Enter your name and choose your country. Your country selection stays locked for the week.
  2. Choose Play for your country to find a rival from another country.
  3. Control your national team through two 60-second halves. Score more goals to win.
  4. After the result is verified, your country receives its league points. Share the score and challenge your friends.

The world table resets weekly on Monday at 00:00 in Türkiye time. Ties are separated by goal difference, goals scored and wins, with country code as the final tie-break.

Play with friends

Choose Play with a Friend in the lobby to create a room. Share its link through your phone’s share menu, WhatsApp or Copy. Your friend can open the link or enter the six-character room code using Join with Code.

Each person controls one full national team. Friends representing the same country can still play, with alternate kits to keep the teams distinguishable.

What if nobody else is searching?

Computer-controlled opponents can fill empty searches after roughly 8–10 seconds. These completed matches count for both countries after the server verifies the result. New players start on Rookie; difficulty adjusts between matches using recent verified bot results. Difficulty never changes during a match.

Share your result

At full time, choose Share Your Result, post to WhatsApp or X, copy your score and game link, or save a PNG scorecard for a social post or story. The scorecard includes both teams and the final score.
