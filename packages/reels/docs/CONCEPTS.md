# HNC social concept bank

Recurring cast: **Emre** (🇹🇷 #9) and **Nikos** (🇬🇷 #4) — same HNC people in
every world, so the feed becomes a running rivalry, not a stream of ads. Swap
countries freely; identity is data.

Every concept answers: what is the viewer wondering after 1s, what changes at
3s, what escalates, what is the surprise, what is the payoff, why would they
send it to a friend, and why it could only be HNC League.

## Demo choice

- **A — New Coworker**: the brief's required office → football piece; proves multi-world stories, character continuity (office worker → #9 → office in kit) and a *motivated* world transformation (fluorescent → floodlight, same framing).
- **B — The Group Chat**: completely different structure — starts in a 2D phone UI, the push notification is the portal (zoom-through), a comedic freeze-frame, and a return to the chat for the punchline.
- **C — Breaking News**: proves content without gameplay — a third world (studio), broadcast graphics, an in-world screen, and the real World Table mechanic as the story.
- Plus **Greece Just Passed You** as a JSON-only spec: the "next scenario is cheap" proof.
